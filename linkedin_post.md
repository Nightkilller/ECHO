# 🚀 Say Hello to ECHO: The Smart Voice-Guided macOS Screen Companion! 🎙️✨

Tired of endless hunting for folders and clicking around your screen? What if your cursor had a mind of its own—and could navigate your workspace for you?

I’m excited to share a project I’ve been building: **ECHO**, an intelligent macOS application designed to bring hands-free precision and smart navigation to your desktop.

---

### 🌟 What is ECHO?
ECHO is a voice/text-guided desktop companion that runs natively on macOS. It helps you navigate your screen, locate specific files or folders, control your workspace windows, and launch applications without you lifting a finger.

### ⚡ What Makes It Different & What's New?
Here’s a breakdown of the new features and custom mechanics we just implemented:
*   🔍 **Circular Magnifying Spotlight Lens**: When finding a file or folder, ECHO uses a native, high-contrast spotlight overlay with a **1.3x circular magnification lens** to instantly highlight exactly where it is on the screen.
*   📐 **Precise Window Controls**: Programmatic edge-snapping, tiling (left/right screen split), maximizing, and minimizing commands that snap your active windows perfectly into place.
*   🎭 **Native Genie & Scale Animations**: Supports native macOS Dock unminimization! Minimized applications glide back onto the screen using macOS's premium native transition effects.
*   🫧 **Status-Bar Attached Setup Panel**: A beautiful, floating menu bar onboarding card that stays persistent and pinned above other applications while granting system permissions.
*   🍎 **Spotlight-Powered App Launching**: Smart casing-agnostic app detection (e.g., launching "spotify" vs "Spotify") utilizing macOS metadata.
